# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and defects with technical clarity and respect.
- Critique work, never person.
- Accept correction and provide evidence for claims.
- Protect private vault data, filesystem paths, credentials, and vulnerability details.
- Respect differing experience, identity, background, and communication style.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Scope

Policy applies in repository, issue tracker, review threads, release discussions, and project-related
public communication.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through
[GitHub security advisory form](https://github.com/pan-narrans/octarine/security/advisories/new).
Include links and factual context. Maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or severe abuse. Reports receive
confidential and proportionate review.
